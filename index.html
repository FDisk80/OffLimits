What's New in v2.6

  • Clean GPS Lock Status: Removed extra accuracy meters, coordinates, and heading text from top-left. Now displays a pristine, minimal "GPS Lock".
  • Alternating AMOLED Pixel Mesh: Hardware-accelerated subpixel checkerboard shader switches adjacent pixels off (pure pitch-black #000000). Every 20s active and dark pixels swap roles, giving 50% rest time to diodes in the large white & red sign.
  • Ultra-Black Stencil Mode: New option to turn the large white sign center 100% pitch black with a glowing red ring and white digits (cuts 85% of active diodes).
  • OpenStreetMap Database Engine: Direct real-time OSM tags (70, 90, 100, 110 km/h) without guessing.
  • High-Frequency Pulls: Refreshes every 4 seconds or 35 meters when driving.
